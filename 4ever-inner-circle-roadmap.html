Phased Roadmap

Build the smallest beautiful thing first.

Then let it earn its way to more.

A clear, honest plan for bringing the Inner Circle to life — built as a Progressive Web App first (installable to the home screen, feels like an app, opens like an app), with a thoughtful path to a native iOS & Android app only when usage justifies it.

Three phases, twelve months, one quiet promise: every part you ship should already feel like 4Ever Photos before the next part is added.

Guiding principle
"Begin with the experience that matters most — a couple, their people, the right message at the right moment. Everything else is added only when it earns the right to be."
— The North Star

The freedom of "later"

By building only what's been earned, Phase 3 stays optional. You can stop at Phase 2 and still have an outstanding product. You can pick one module from Phase 3 and skip the others. The roadmap serves you, not the other way around.

The decision gates

How you know it's time to move on

Each phase only begins when the previous one has proved itself. No moving forward until couples are actually using what you've built.

Phase 1 ↓ Phase 2

Before you build the depth

  • At least 5 real couples have used the Inner Circle end-to-end from booking through wedding day.
  • The role-based messaging is landing — the bridal party and parents are reading and responding.
  • At least 2 couples have installed the PWA to their home screen.
  • The Edit (Shopify) has generated at least 10 orders.
  • You've collected meaningful feedback on what couples wished was there but wasn't.
Phase 2 ↓ Phase 3 (native)

Before you build the native app

  • PWA install rate is above 40% — couples are choosing "Add to Home Screen" without prompting.
  • Couples are opening the Inner Circle at least weekly during their planning months.
  • There is a specific feature you genuinely cannot deliver well as a web app (heavy camera, AR, deep wallet integration).
  • You have the revenue base to support ongoing native maintenance — app store fees, OS updates twice a year, dual codebase.
Risks & dependencies

What to watch honestly

Every project has them. Naming them now means none of them surprise you later.

Phase 2 · Plan ahead
WhatsApp Business approval

Meta's approval window is typically 4–8 weeks. Begin the application in month 1 even though it won't be used until Phase 2. Template messages must be pre-approved.

Phase 1 · Design-led
Brand integrity in Shopify embed

Shopify's default styling does not match your brand. The embed needs careful theming so The Edit feels like the Inner Circle, not a shop bolted on.

Phase 1 · Non-negotiable
GDPR from day one

Adding GDPR after launch is painful and expensive. Designed in from the start, it's a feature. Cookie consent, data export, deletion, supplier data sharing terms.

Phase 2 · External dependency
Bodytap voucher readiness

The Calm Corner depends on Bodytap being able to accept and redeem coins. If Bodytap isn't ready, the voucher feature can stub for Phase 2 and integrate properly in Phase 3.

Phase 3 · Strategic
The "native app" temptation

It's tempting to build native sooner because it feels premium. Resist unless the data is clear. A beloved PWA outperforms a shipped-too-early native app every time.

All phases · Operational
The content burden

Beautifully written role-based messages don't write themselves. Plan for a content sprint before Phase 1 launch — best man tips, bridesmaid notes, supplier briefs, all in your voice.
